QUESTIONS

Understand what you’re looking at.

Is this the original Market Strength website?

No. This is an independent reconstruction based on public pages and observed UI behavior. It has no connection to the original operator.

Is the data real?

The built-in 40-stock dataset is synthetic and reproducible. Use Yahoo Finance in the scanner to load real daily histories, or import your own daily OHLCV CSV.

What is reconstructed?

The local app includes RS rankings, moving averages, stages, trend-template checks, sector quadrants, four scans, watchlists, chart controls, CSV import/export, a trade journal, and a momentum backtester. Yahoo Finance daily history is available through the local server. Auth, cloud alerts, AI services and proprietary pattern detectors are not connected.

Can I use these ratings as investment advice?

No. These are mechanical research outputs. Imported data may be incomplete, and simulation results depend on the supplied universe and assumptions.
